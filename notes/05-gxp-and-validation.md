# GxP, validation and audit readiness
GxP is the family of good practice regulations in life sciences: Good Manufacturing Practice, Good Clinical Practice and Good Laboratory Practice. Systems that touch regulated data must be validated.
Computer system validation shows that a system does what it is meant to, consistently, with documented evidence. GAMP 5 is the common framework and takes a risk-based approach.
Audit readiness means traceability from requirement to test to release, controlled change, and data integrity following ALCOA+: attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate.
For AI in regulated settings, the model version, training data and evaluation results must be traceable, and changes go through change control.
