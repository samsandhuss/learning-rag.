# Platform product ownership
A platform product owner treats internal engineers as customers. Success is measured by adoption, self-service uptake and outcomes, not features shipped.
Prioritise the backlog by value, risk and reliability. Link every item to an OKR and a named metric. Say no with a reason when an item does not compete on value.
Flow metrics such as throughput, cycle time and work in progress show delivery health. Act on them rather than just reporting them.
Reliability is agreed with SRE through SLOs and error budgets. When the error budget is spent, reliability work comes before new features.
FinOps brings cloud cost into product decisions, so cost per team or per workload becomes a backlog input.
